# Model-structure audit

**Script:** `Script_Bank/Analysis/SRM_AND_SBI_MONOMER_DIMER_ALP_Model_Structure_Audit.py`
**Output:** `<data_bank_root>/Posit/SRM_AND_SBI_MONOMER_DIMER_ALP_Model_Structure_Audit/` (report `.md` + `audit_summary.json`)
**Scope:** the separated stoichiometry–mobility generator introduced in 0.1.2, made condition-aware in 0.1.3, and given its decided prior ranges and declared occupancies in 0.1.4 (two molecular species × three mobility modes = six particle types; the reaction channels generated from the blocks and the condition's declared association ratio, seventeen under MET-INLB and eleven under MET-FAB; eleven learnable parameters shared by both conditions, the initial composition as the log dimer-to-monomer ratio; the shared parameter-conversion rule; one trajectory tier per condition; per-condition declared occupancy). A correctness check of the model STRUCTURE and of the decided table, not an inference validation. The companion **prior-realization audit** (`..._Prior_Realization_Audit.py`) checks GENERATED products against the prior and the declared inputs.

## Why it exists

Subunit conservation alone cannot detect a wrong reaction channel: a network that sends two slow monomers into a fast dimer conserves subunits just as well as the intended one. The model specification's implementation increment therefore asks for deterministic checks that run without a simulation, before any approved run, and a few tiny run-level checks after approval. This script is that check, in the same shape as the labeling audit: prespecified criteria, one report, every number in a JSON.

## Deterministic tier (no simulation; always runs)

| check | criterion |
|---|---|
| D1 channels | Per condition, `reaction_channels(theta, condition)` yields exactly the declared channels. MET-INLB (association ratio 1): 17 unique channels — 6 association fusions (one per unordered pair of monomer modes; product = dimer in the SLOWER parent's mode; one shared microscopic rate λ_on = λ_ref), 3 dissociation fissions (dimer → two monomers in the dimer's mode), 8 switching conversions (four shared rates × two species; adjacent modes only). MET-FAB (association ratio 0): 11 channels — NO fusion channel at all (a structural setting, not a tiny rate), the same fissions and conversions. `build_system` registers exactly these names under each condition. |
| D2 diffusion | `D[X, m] = D_A · species_factor · mode_factor` for every type; on 20 000 prior draws plus every box corner, immobile < slow ≤ fast within a species and dimer ≤ monomer within a mode (the disjoint-range guarantee checked at import). |
| D3 transforms | `to_flow(to_physical(u)) == u` on prior draws and both box corners; every row of the decided table is a log row, so a blanket `10**u` equals `to_physical` everywhere; the per-row rule still passes a synthetic linear row through untouched (the contract stays general); every table `VALUE` is its row's prior center. |
| D4 composition | `realize_initial_composition` from the dimer-to-monomer ratio r for totals 1..40 and the box floor and ceiling (316, 3162) at r ∈ {0, 0.01, 1, 100, huge}: conservation N_R = n_A + 2 n_B, dimer cap ⌊N_R/2⌋, dimers monotone in r, the derived x_B = 2r/(1+2r) and f_B = r/(1+r) as documented (r = 1 ↔ f_B = 1/2 ↔ x_B = 2/3), at least one dimer at the box floor (three at N_R = 316, r = 0.01); non-integer totals round, sub-one totals floor to one monomer, a negative ratio is rejected. The association reference equals 4π(2D_A)r / ((4/3)πr³) = 6 D_A / r²; under MET-INLB every fusion fires at exactly λ_ref (R_ON = 1), and under MET-FAB no fusion channel exists. |
| D5 stationary law | `stationary_mode_law` sums to one and satisfies detailed balance on every link, over 200 prior draws. |
| D6 occupancy | `rank_to_species` maps every monomer type to A and every dimer type to B; a per-species occupancy yields identical probabilities for A_f, A_s, A_i (and for B_f, B_s, B_i); an occupancy keyed by particle type is rejected; a trajectory carrying the legacy A/B/C types is rejected. |
| D7 fission placement | Fission daughters are placed at `SimulationStem.fission_product_distance_nm` = 2 × the reaction distance (20 nm), outside the fusion radius, and `build_system` passes that field. Declared convention (2026-09-09): at the 2 ms sub-step an eligible pair reacts with probability ≈ 1 for every D_A in range, so daughters placed AT the radius would re-fuse at the next step unless they diffused apart within one step (≈ 3% for fast, ≈ 50% per step for immobile daughters at the prior center), making the effective unbinding rate mode dependent. The one-step rebinding probabilities under the old placement are reported as the documented reason. |
| D8 condition registry | The model's condition tokens equal the labeling registry's and the experiment registry's; the MET-FAB association ratio is exactly 0.0 and the MET-INLB ratio exactly 1.0; the retired `relative_rate_dimerization` row is absent from the biology table and from the detector's RDS nuisance; a small positive ratio is refused as a disguised zero; `Paths.rds_alias` carries the condition token, refuses to resolve without one, and resolves to the same tier for both workflows. Declared occupancies: MET-INLB 0.5 (declared; visibility 0.25), MET-FAB 0.155 (DERIVED = 0.5 × 0.25 / 0.806 from the declared Fab/InlB visibility ratio 0.5 and the InlB anchor; visibility 0.125); a condition setting with both or neither of `occupancy` and `visibility_ratio` is refused. |
| D10 theta schema | A Theta_Set written by `io.write_theta_set` (`.zarr` and `.npy`) carries the table's schema and reads back through `io.load_theta_set`; a table with a renamed key, a table with a shifted prior bound, a different condition, a schema-less `.zarr`, and a `.npy` without its sidecar are each refused (`ThetaSetSchemaError`), and `theta_set_status` names the reason. | PASS |
| D9 decided ranges | A literal copy of the ranges decided on 2026-09-14 equals the parameterization's table (an undecided edit fails here). Immobility by construction: R_i · D_A at the D_A center stays below the pipelines' looser immobility threshold (0.0065 µm²/s) for every R_i in range (whether it also stays below the stricter 0.0028 at the D_A ceiling is reported, not required); the slow factor's floor is at least one decade above the immobile factor's ceiling; the dissociation floor (0.001 per s) loses at most 2% of dimers over 20 s; the composition box is symmetric about an even split. |

## Run tier (`--run`; tiny simulations; explicit approval required)

| check | criterion |
|---|---|
| R1 conservation | One 1 s run per condition at the prior center (MET-INLB: the full reactive network; MET-FAB: dissociation and switching only): the lineage replays with every subunit covered once per frame (the extractor fails loud otherwise) and its subunit count equals the realized N_R; the per-frame subunit total from the particles observable is constant. |
| R2 stationarity | The isolated switching chain under the MET-FAB configuration (no association channel by construction; monomers only, 600 particles, 10 s): time-averaged mode occupancies over the second half within 0.03 of `stationary_mode_law`. |
| R3 visible fractions | 400 static labelings on the MET-INLB lineage (every channel exercised) at each condition's DECLARED occupancy under its own law: monomer `a = p_occ (1 − P0)`, dimer `1 − (1 − a)²`, and the both-labeled share among visible dimers `a / (2 − a)` within 0.02; the occupancy applied is recorded in the labeling row. |
| R4 both conditions | One 20-frame video per condition rendered from THAT condition's own trajectory through the production renderer; both carry finite signal. |
| R5 boundary | In the MET-INLB run, particles may lie outside the imaged field (open lateral boundary, no confining potential) while the subunit total stays constant; the fraction outside is reported, not judged. |
| R6 timing | One 2 s MET-INLB run at the most particles the decided box allows (N_R = 3162, r = 0.01 → 3100 monomers + 31 dimers, D_A at its ceiling): wall time reported as the compute check of the count range; descriptive, never a failure. |

## Reading the report

A deterministic FAIL is a structural defect in the generator or the table and blocks everything downstream; a D9 FAIL means the table no longer equals the decided ranges. A run-tier FAIL in R1 is a lineage or network defect; in R2 a switching-rate or initial-mode defect; in R3 an occupancy or labeling defect; in R4 a renderer/lineage handoff defect; R5 and R6 are descriptive. The JSON carries the channel list, the per-check numbers, and the tolerances.
