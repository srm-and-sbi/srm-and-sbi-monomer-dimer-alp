# Model-structure audit

**Script:** `Script_Bank/Analysis/SRM_AND_SBI_MONOMER_DIMER_ALP_Model_Structure_Audit.py`
**Output:** `<data_bank_root>/Posit/SRM_AND_SBI_MONOMER_DIMER_ALP_Model_Structure_Audit/` (report `.md` + `audit_summary.json`)
**Scope:** the separated stoichiometry–mobility generator and its window-start composition. Two molecular species under MET-FAB and three under MET-INLB, each in three mobility modes (six or nine particle types); the reaction channels generated from the blocks and the condition's declared association ratio, twenty-four under MET-INLB and eleven under MET-FAB; the per-condition parameter tables, eleven learnable rows under MET-FAB and twelve under MET-INLB, which also infers the initial active-dimer allocation R_active (MET-FAB declares it 0); the reference probe-bound population and the window-start allocation (`parameterization.realize_population`); the shared parameter-conversion rule with its two linear fraction rows; one trajectory tier per condition; the per-condition occupancies. A correctness check of the model STRUCTURE and of the decided tables, not an inference validation. The companion **prior-realization audit** (`..._Prior_Realization_Audit.py`) checks GENERATED products against the prior and the declared inputs.

## Why it exists

Subunit conservation alone cannot detect a wrong reaction channel: a network that sends two slow monomers into a fast dimer conserves subunits just as well as the intended one. The model specification's implementation increment therefore asks for deterministic checks that run without a simulation, before any approved run, and a few tiny run-level checks after approval. This script is that check, in the same shape as the labeling audit: prespecified criteria, one report, every number in a JSON.

## Deterministic tier (no simulation; always runs)

| check | criterion |
|---|---|
| D1 channels | Per condition, `reaction_channels(theta, condition)` at the condition's prior center yields exactly the declared channels. MET-FAB (association ratio 0): 11 channels — NO fusion channel at all (a structural setting, not a tiny rate), 3 fissions B → A + A, 8 switching conversions. MET-INLB (association ratio 1, ligand classes B1 / B2): 24 channels — 6 association fusions A + A → B2 (one per unordered pair of monomer modes; product in the SLOWER parent's mode; one shared microscopic rate λ_on = λ_ref), 3 fissions B2 → A + A, 3 conversions B1 → A at κ_OFF (the probe-free partner is not represented), 12 switching conversions (the four shared rates, once per species A, B1, B2; adjacent modes only). Every channel conserves the represented subunit count; `build_system` registers exactly these names under each condition. |
| D2 diffusion | Per condition, `D[X, m] = D_A · species_factor · mode_factor` for every type; on 20,000 prior draws plus every box corner, immobile < slow ≤ fast within a species and dimer ≤ monomer within a mode (the disjoint-range guarantee checked at import). |
| D3 transforms | Per condition, `to_flow(to_physical(u, table), table) == u` on prior draws and both box corners. The linear rows of the condition's table are exactly {f} (MET-FAB) and {f, R_active} (MET-INLB) and pass through unchanged, so a blanket `10**u` differs from `to_physical` on those columns (and equals it on the log columns); `to_physical`, `to_flow` and `fraction_flags` refuse a call without a table, and the conversion refuses a theta of the wrong width; every table `VALUE` is its row's prior center; no prior draw is flagged by `fraction_flags`. |
| D4 reference population | Per condition, at zero allocation (the declared 0 under MET-FAB), the integers of `realize_population` equal an audit-local literal of the ratio form EXACTLY. With r = f / (1 − f): n_A,true = N / (1 + 2r), n_B,true = r N / (1 + 2r), D_sim = round(p (2 − p) n_B,true), B2 = min(round(p² n_B,true), D_sim), A = round(p n_A,true), rounded half to even, with the limits n_A,true = 0 and n_B,true = N / 2 at f = 1. The cases: a 41 × 101 grid over N_total ∈ [1,000, 100,000] (log-spaced) × f ∈ [0, 1] (both endpoints) and 100,000 random draws, 208,282 per condition. The represented receptor count N_R = A + 2 D_sim (MET-FAB: both subunits of a Fab dimer are represented) or A + B1 + 2 B2 (MET-INLB) lies within 1.5 subunits of its expectation; D_sim is monotone in f, with no dimer at f = 0 and no monomer at f = 1. An invalid input (N_total ≤ 0, f or R_active non-finite or outside [0, 1], a nonzero MET-FAB allocation, an unknown condition) is refused on both paths (`realize_population`, `realize_initial_composition`) with nothing returned, and a positional call of the keyword-only initializer raises `TypeError`. The ceilings at N_total = 100,000 are reported, not judged: MET-FAB places 1,477 particles at f = 0, 1,472 at f = 0.25 and 1,466 at f = 1, while N_R rises from 1,477 through 2,058 to 2,932 (more probe-free partners are represented inside dimers) and the probe-bound receptors stay at about 1,477, reported apart; MET-INLB keeps N_R at 4,761–4,762 in 4,762, 4,716 and 4,649 particles. The association reference equals 4π(2D_A)r / ((4/3)πr³) = 6 D_A / r²; under MET-INLB every fusion fires at exactly λ_ref (R_ON = 1), and under MET-FAB no fusion channel exists. |
| D5 stationary law | Per condition, `stationary_mode_law` sums to one and satisfies detailed balance on every link, over 200 prior draws. |
| D6 probe rule | `rank_to_species` maps every monomer type to the stoichiometric class A and every dimer type (B, B1, B2) to B, and `rank_to_molecule` keeps the ligand class; the probe rule of `labeling.assign_probes` binds every represented monomer, every subunit of a B1 / B2 host, and one or two subunits of a Fab dimer at the declared two-probe share; an override's per-class coins give identical probabilities within a class; an occupancy keyed by particle type is rejected; a trajectory carrying the legacy A/B/C types is rejected. |
| D7 fission placement | Fission daughters are placed `SimulationStem.fission_product_distance_nm` = 4 × the reaction distance apart (40 nm; 2 × until 2026-10-13) and `build_system` passes that field. ReaDDy orients the daughter axis uniformly in three dimensions inside the 2 nm periodic slab, so a fraction `1 − sqrt(1 − (r/d)²)` of the daughter pairs lies within the reaction distance in the plane: 13.4 % at 20 nm, 3.2 % at 40 nm, the accepted residual (the criterion requires the geometric fraction ≤ 4 %). Reported beside it: the next-step re-fusion fraction per daughter mode at the prior center and the one-step rebinding probability under placement AT the radius (≈ 50 % per step for immobile daughters), the reason for placing the daughters apart at all. |
| D8 condition registry | The model's condition tokens equal the labeling registry's and the experiment registry's; the MET-FAB association ratio is exactly 0.0 and the MET-INLB ratio exactly 1.0. The retired rows (`relative_rate_dimerization` and the log dimer-to-monomer ratio `ratio_dimer_monomer_initial`) are absent from both conditions' tables, from the master table and from the detector's RDS-nuisance mirror, which equals the twelve master keys; the MET-FAB keys equal the MET-INLB keys without the allocation, the last one (alignment is by key everywhere; the shared prefix is a safety margin); MET-FAB declares the allocation 0 and MET-INLB infers it; the condition-free table names and the ratio helpers are absent from `parameterization`. Occupancies of the underlying population: MET-INLB 0.0476 (equilibrium at 0.25 nM with K_D 5 nM), MET-FAB 0.0148 (derived from the declared Fab/InlB fluorescence ratio 0.5 and the InlB anchor); per-subunit fluorescence probabilities 0.0238 / 0.0119; two-probe shares 0.0244 / 0.0074; MET-INLB carries the ligand classes and MET-FAB does not. | D9 decided ranges and scales | A literal copy of the decided ranges and scales equals each condition's table, in master order (an undecided edit fails here): the count row log10 [3, 5], the basal reference fraction linear on (0, 1), the dissociation rate log10 [−3, 1], the mobility rows, and the allocation linear on (0, 1), present only under MET-INLB. Immobility by construction: R_i · D_A at the D_A center stays below the pipelines' looser immobility threshold (0.0065 µm²/s) for every R_i in range (whether it also stays below the stricter 0.0028 at the D_A ceiling is reported, not required); the slow factor's floor is at least one decade above the immobile factor's ceiling; the dissociation floor (0.001 per s) loses at most 2 % of dimers over 20 s. |

## Reading the report

A deterministic FAIL is a structural defect in the generator or the tables and blocks everything downstream: a D9 FAIL means a table no longer equals the decided ranges and scales, a D4 or D12 FAIL that the composition helper no longer realizes the specification. A run-tier FAIL in R1 is a placement, lineage or network defect (the frame-0 counts against the starting counts tell placement apart); in R2 a switching-rate or initial-mode defect; in R3 a probe-rule or labeling defect; in R4 a renderer/lineage handoff defect; R5 and R6 are descriptive. The JSON carries the channel list, the per-check numbers, and the tolerances.
